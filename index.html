IT Student Achievements Portal

Vasavi College of Engineering · Department of Information Technology

Setup required

Connect this portal to your Supabase project by editing the SUPABASE_URL and SUPABASE_ANON_KEY values near the top of app.js. Follow README.md before using this portal.

Sign in

Faculty accounts must be created by the portal administrator and assigned the faculty role.

Student registration

Use a college-approved email address. If email confirmation is enabled in Supabase, confirm your email before signing in.

Submit a participation certificate

Submit one record per event. Upload PDF, JPG, or PNG (maximum 5 MB).

My submissions

Faculty verification

Review student-submitted certificates. Approve or reject each record. Add a reason when rejecting.

Privacy notice: Use only authorised college accounts and legitimate student records. Access is controlled by Supabase authentication and database policies. Configure your institution's data-retention and consent practices before official use.
